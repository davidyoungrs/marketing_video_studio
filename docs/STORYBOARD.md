# Storyboard & Narration Script: Setting Up a Free Digital Business Card

**Feature:** Setting Up a Free Digital Business Card  
**Target Video Format:** 16:9 Landscape (1920 × 1080), 60 FPS / 30 FPS, ~45-60 seconds  
**Tone:** Modern, confident, crisp, professional  
**Audio:** Clear voiceover with subtle background score  

---

## Shot Breakdown & Narration Sync

### Beat 1: The Hook & Landing Page (0:00 - 0:10)
* **Visual:** Genuine browser footage of `https://www.reallysimpleapps.com`. Smooth camera pan over the headline *"Turn Conversations into Conversions"*. Mouse cursor hovers and clicks on **"Start for Free"**.
* **Narration:**
  > "First impressions happen in seconds. With Really Simple, you can replace outdated paper cards with an interactive digital business card in under a minute—completely free."
* **On-Screen Text:** *Create in Seconds • 100% Free*

---

### Beat 2: The Card Builder & Instant Customization (0:10 - 0:25)
* **Visual:** Browser arrives at the card builder (`/create`). We see professional details being typed in real-time (*Sarah Jenkins, Product Lead, Apex Dynamics*). The live smartphone preview card immediately reflects the input dynamically.
* **Narration:**
  > "Just enter your name, role, and bio. Every keystroke updates your live mobile card preview instantly."
* **On-Screen Text:** *Real-Time Live Preview*

---

### Beat 3: Adding Contact Channels & Socials (0:25 - 0:38)
* **Visual:** Smooth scroll down to contact & social channels. Direct phone, email, and LinkedIn are added. Action buttons (*Connect, Save, Wallet*) light up on the card.
* **Narration:**
  > "Add your direct phone, email, and social profiles. Prospects can save your contact details directly to their phone with a single tap."
* **On-Screen Text:** *One-Tap Contact Saving*

---

### Beat 4: Themes & Visual Identity (0:38 - 0:50)
* **Visual:** The mouse cycles through sleek theme palettes: **Midnight**, **Aurora**, and **Sunset**, followed by modern typography selection (**Outfit** / **Inter**). The live card transitions smoothly between styles.
* **Narration:**
  > "Choose from curated designer themes and modern typography to match your personal brand or corporate identity seamlessly."
* **On-Screen Text:** *Curated Designer Themes*

---

### Beat 5: Review & Call to Action (0:50 - 1:00)
* **Visual:** Click on **"Review & Choose Plan"**. Smooth zoom onto the finished card preview with QR and wallet integration ready. Branded closing end-card with logo and URL.
* **Narration:**
  > "Ready to upgrade how you connect? Start for free today at reallysimpleapps.com."
* **On-Screen Text:** *Start for Free at reallysimpleapps.com*
